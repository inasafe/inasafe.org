# Joint EOGC-GIT4NDM 2015 Conference

There will be a pre-conference workshop on “Open source hands-on GIS workshop for disaster management using QGIS and GRASS”  at the CRESCENT BUILDING, UNITED ARAB EMIRATES UNIVERSITY, AL-AIN. The workshop will be delivered by Prof. Scott Madry from the University of North Carolina, USA. 8TH -10TH DECEMBER 2015.
