# For Translators

## Transifex

The web-based translation platform [Transifex](https://www.transifex.com) is used
for InaSAFE translations. So the first thing you need is an account to login and
get started.

### Get an account

1. Go to [www.transifex.com](https://www.transifex.com) and create a new account.
2. Verify your account using the link in the email you will receive.
3. Login.
4. Choose your role as "Translator" and answer some other questions about yourself.
5. Go to the [InaSAFE Transifex project](https://www.transifex.com/projects/p/inasafe-develop/)
   and ask to join the translation.
    - If the language of your choice is listed, click it and request to join the team.
    - If your language is not listed yet, click "Request language" and choose your language.
6. Wait for the maintainers to process your request. You will be notified by email
   when your request has been accepted.
